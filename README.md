## What this list is

A short, hand-checked index of public Chinese-language Telegram communities.
It is not a dump of every channel that exists — it is the subset that is
still active, still public, and still has a description worth reading.

## What this list is not

- Not affiliated with Telegram.
- Not an endorsement. Being listed means the community is public and active,
  not that its content is safe, legal or accurate.
- Not complete. There are far more channels than this; we keep the list short
  on purpose.

## How entries are selected

An entry is listed only when all of the following hold:

1. It is a public channel, group or bot with a real description — not just a
   slogan, a contact address, or a wall of cross-promo links.
2. It has at least 3,000 subscribers (channels and groups) or a non-zero
   monthly user count (bots).
3. It was re-verified within the last 30 days.
4. It carries no risk flag for adult content, gambling, paid stock tips,
   invite-code resale or proxy/VPN services.
5. It is not a near-duplicate of another entry from the same operator.

Every summary is hand-written after reading the community's own description,
not copy-pasted from it. If a description is too thin, or is mostly promo
text and channel-swapping links, the entry is dropped rather than padded.
Summaries stay in Chinese on purpose — translating a community's own
self-description tends to lose the exact nuance that makes it useful.

## Why the links point to ChanScout

Every link goes to a ChanScout profile page instead of a raw `t.me` link.
Channels get renamed, go private or disappear — a list of raw invite links is
half dead within six months. The profile page stays current, and returns 410
once an entry is permanently removed. If you only want the direct link, it is
the first thing on that page.

Self-submissions are not accepted — entries are chosen by the maintainer, so
the list does not turn into a link farm. Open an issue instead of a pull
request: both READMEs are generated files and edits to them get overwritten.
The full criteria are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
